FAQ

Good to know

Is this a real medical platform?

No. MEDICORE is a frontend portfolio project. It does not store or process real patient medical records, and it is not HIPAA or GDPR compliant.

Is patient data real?

All patient, doctor and appointment data is fictional demo content stored only in your browser's local storage.

Where does the health data come from?

Country-level statistics in Global Health Intelligence come from the World Bank's public Open Data API, credited wherever shown.

Do I need an account?

No real account is required — use the role switcher to explore the patient, doctor and admin experiences instantly.

See the whole platform in minutes.

No sign-up required — jump straight into a demo account and switch roles as you go.
